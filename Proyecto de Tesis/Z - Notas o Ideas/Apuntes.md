\item \textbf{Operation-Time Design Automation:} In \cite{Saidi2022AutonomousSystems}, the authors posit that self-design requires obscuring the boundary between the design phase (the laboratory) and the autonomous operation phase (the field). They propose migrating Design Automation (DA) methods—such as Design Space Exploration (DSE), synthesis, and online verification—to the operation phase. This continuous execution enables the system to autonomously evaluate, optimize, and safeguard its evolving architecture dynamically.

\item \textbf{Knowledge-Driven Composition:} In distributed domains such as the Internet of Things (IoT), self-design is achieved through dynamic semantic integration. Methodologies like those proposed in \cite{noauthor_2019-px} and \cite{BanerjeeAmarAKnowledgeDriven} utilize capability ontologies and knowledge bases to formalize the functions of heterogeneous devices. By reasoning over these models, the system autonomously synthesizes control architectures, promoting a bottom-up architectural evolution that overcomes the constraints of predefined, top-down integration standards.
