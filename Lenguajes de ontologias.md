An ontology can be defined by the ontology representation languages such as RDF(S), SHOE, OIL, DAML, DAML+OIL, and OWL (Horrocks et al. 2003). Among them, the most widely used is OWL, which is a W3C standard for expressing ontologies in the Semantic Web. OWL has three increasingly expressive sublanguages OWL Lite, OWL DL, and OWL Full. OWL Full has the highest expressiveness while it has been proved to be undecidable (Motik 2005).
